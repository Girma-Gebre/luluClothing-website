LULU CLOTHING

Slow Fashion, Handcrafted for Life

LULU clothing is a popular women's boutique located on the ground floor of the Medhane Alem Mall (right next to Edna Mall) in Addis Ababa. The store specializes in trendy women's apparel, dresses, fashion accessories, and cosmetics.

We design all collections natively in-house, working side-by-side with local ethical mills and independent women-led artisan collectives worldwide. Our focus remains centered on beautiful drape work, high durability, and organic luxury fabric compositions.

Our Mission

To design elegant, high-performance garments that instill profound confidence in modern women, while advocating for and maintaining absolute transparency, non-toxic organic sourcing, and fair-wage craftsmanship globally.

Our Vision

To transition the global fashion community away from trend-induced fast production and establish LULU as an international benchmark for aesthetic-forward, circular-economy luxury apparel.
